HOLD OUTPOST ECHO.

Destroy the fictional undead horde before it crosses the illuminated wire. Every breach damages the shared outpost. Zero integrity ends the run.

  1. AC-130 gunship: top-down thermal view. Use 1 / 2 / 3 to select the 25 mm, 40 mm or 105 mm weapon. Use the 40 mm for rapid cluster-clearing and the 105 mm for a massive blast over a much wider area.
  2. Artillery: lead approaching groups. The landing ring marks where a shell will impact after its flight.
  3. M2 .50 cal: defend from ground level. Fire short bursts, watch heat and reload the belt between groups.

Work the battlefield: orange fuel drums trigger chain blasts and award 25 points each. Heavy explosions stagger survivors; bulwarks resist knockback. Aim at heads with the 25 mm or M2 for 1.65× direct damage. Enemies steer around buildings, so watch their path before firing delayed shells.

After each round, choose one of three upgrades for the station you just used. It stays installed when that station returns. The outpost automatically recovers 8 integrity and weapons are resupplied. Hordes grow every round; runners arrive in round 2, bulwarks from round 3.

Display: use the top-bar selector to fit the whole battlefield and controls to the window, enter fullscreen, or use a scrolling page. Changes pause combat. Resume when ready. Leaving fullscreen restores your windowed choice.

Controls: point and hold left mouse / Space to fire; arrows or WASD move the aim; R reloads; P or Esc pauses. On touch screens, drag the battlefield to aim and hold FIRE. A connected standard gamepad uses the left stick, right trigger or A to fire, X to reload, shoulders to select and Start to pause.

Last Light March: an original 120 BPM military-style score with field snares, marching bass drums and brass-style calls. Each station gets its own deterministic arrangement. Choose any arrangement below to export the complete 32-second WAV.

Leaving the window pauses your run. Sound starts only after deployment. Your best score and sound preferences stay on this device. A local server is needed for downloaded ES modules; after the first full visit, offline play is available.

An original arcade game. No Call of Duty assets, music or code. Not a real weapon simulation.
